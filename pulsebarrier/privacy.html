2. The essentials first

Pulse Barrier is a single-player game without a user account, without our own server, without ads, without purchases and without any analytics or crash reporting service built in by us. Your save game and your settings are stored only on your device. The game has no permission to access the internet and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

We only receive data about you in three cases: when you take part in the closed test (section 3.4), when you write to us or send feedback through Google Play (section 3.5), and as statistics that Google Play provides to us about installations and crashes of the app (section 3.3).

You are not required by law or contract to provide any data.

3. Processing in detail

3.1 Save game, profiles and settings

The game stores on your device your progress (levels completed, stars, best results, research, achievements, codex, game statistics such as games won and lost), your settings (language, volume, vibration, flashes, camera shake, motion, health bars, automatic pause), up to six player profiles and the date of the version of the terms of use that you accepted in the game. You can give a profile a name; this is optional, a nickname is enough. On start, the game reads your device's system language to choose German or English when the language setting is "System"; the system language itself is not stored. The data is kept in the app's protected storage, which other apps cannot access. The game only uses it to show you your progress and your settings and does not send it to anyone.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the game you use). Storing data on your device is strictly necessary for the game (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG).

3.2 No Android backup

The game is excluded from Android's automatic backup. Your save game is therefore not backed up to your Google account. It is lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data. When you switch devices, Android usually does not transfer it; during a direct transfer to a new device (by cable or Wi-Fi) it may be transferred, depending on the manufacturer. It does not leave your devices in the process.

3.3 Distribution via Google Play

You download the game from Google Play. Google processes data about your device and your Google account under its own responsibility in accordance with Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). In the Google Play Console, Google provides us with statistics, such as the number of installations by country, device model and Android version, as well as crash and error reports (Android vitals). Crash reports contain technical information such as device model, Android version, app version and the program flow at the time of the crash, but no name and no e-mail address. According to Google, they only come from devices whose users have allowed Google to share usage and diagnostics data. Because only a few people take part in the test, we cannot rule out that a report could be linked to a tester via the device model. We do not attempt this and only use the information to find bugs and to keep the game running on common devices.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in detecting and fixing bugs in the game).

3.4 Taking part in the closed test

You take part in the closed test at our invitation. For this, we add the e-mail address of your Google account to the tester list in the Google Play Console, or you join a Google Group that we have set up for this purpose. Google then makes the game available to this account. We only use the tester list to manage access to the test and, where necessary, to inform you about the test.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (carrying out your participation under the Terms of Use for the closed test). We remove your address from the tester list as soon as you ask us to, and no later than three months after the end of the test. The test ends when the game is generally released or when we announce that it has been discontinued.

3.5 Feedback and e-mails

If you write to us at the address above or send us feedback on the test through Google Play, we process your e-mail address or the name of your Google account and the content of your message, and for feedback via Google Play also the device model, Android version, app version and language supplied by Google. We use this to reply to you and to improve the game. Please do not send us screenshots that contain personal information, such as notifications from other apps. Our mailbox is operated by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany. We delete e-mails once the request has been dealt with, and feedback on the test no later than three months after the end of the test, unless a retention obligation applies. Feedback via Google Play is stored by Google under its own rules; we do not copy anything from it into our own records except a summary without names.
Legal basis: for requests concerning your data subject rights Art. 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Art. 12 GDPR, for feedback on the test Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in answering questions about our game).

3.6 Deletion

You can delete individual profiles in the game under "Profiles". You can remove all of the game's data in the app's Android settings ("Storage" – "Clear data") or by uninstalling it.

3.7 Data security

Your data is kept in the app's protected storage and, on current devices, is additionally covered by Android's encryption. The game itself does not transmit anything over the internet.

4. Recipients

  • Google: distribution via Google Play, management of the test, statistics and crash reports (sections 3.3 and 3.4). Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and other Google companies in accordance with Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany: operator of our e-mail mailbox (section 3.5).

There are no other recipients. We do not sell data and do not pass any on to advertising or analytics services.

5. Transfers to third countries

Google may also process data from distribution, the test and the statistics in the USA. According to Google, the transfer is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and additionally on standard contractual clauses; we have not verified this ourselves.

6. Retention

  • Save game, profiles and settings: on your device until you delete them in the game, clear the app data or uninstall the app.
  • Tester list: until you ask for removal, no later than three months after the end of the test.
  • Feedback and e-mails: as described in section 3.5.
  • Statistics and crash reports in the Google Play Console: according to Google's rules, which we cannot influence.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Please contact us at the e-mail address above. At most, we hold your address in the tester list and messages you have sent us. You can delete the data on your device yourself (section 3.6). Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for us: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, https://www.datenschutz-berlin.de

8. No automated decision-making

There is no profiling and no automated decision-making.

9. App permissions

  • Vibration: for vibration feedback, which you can switch off in the settings.

The game has no other permissions, in particular none for internet access, the camera, the microphone, location, contacts or your photos and files. You can check this in the Android settings under Apps → Pulse Barrier → Permissions. The game does not use the advertising ID.

10. Children

Only people aged 18 and over can take part in the closed test. The later version of the game is not directed at children under 13 either.

11. Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, the new version will be shipped with the next release and linked in the Play Store.
