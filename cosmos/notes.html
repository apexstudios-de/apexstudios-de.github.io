Apex Cosmos – About the app

About what you are buying

The app itself is free. Purchasing "Apex Cosmos Pro" inside the app gives you the software by Apex Studios and its full range of features only – fetching, processing, calculating and displaying. The images and data shown come from public archives and services (including NASA, ESA/Hubble, ESA/Webb, ESO, NOAA, USGS, CelesTrak and the PALEOMAP Project). They are not sold; Apex Studios claims no rights in them, and a purchase neither extends nor restricts any rights in them. Each image is subject to the terms of its source, as listed in the app's image credits.
